Privacy Policy

1. What we collect

When you book a repair we may collect your name, phone number, email address and details about your device and the fault. This is the information needed to carry out the repair and let you know when it is done.

2. How we use it

Contact details are used to update you on your repair, confirm quotes and arrange collection. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

3. Devices and stored data

Repair work sometimes requires a device to be powered on and tested. We only access what is needed to confirm the repair works, and we recommend removing or backing up sensitive material before drop-off.

4. Website

This website does not use contact forms and does not collect personal information directly. Standard server logs may record technical details such as browser type for security purposes. The embedded Google Map is provided by Google and is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

5. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you may ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you and request that it be corrected or deleted.

6. Contact

For any privacy questions, ring us on 027 573 8028 or use the contact details on the main page.
