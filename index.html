FAQ

Before you bring it in

Can't see your question? Give us a call on 027 573 8028.

What can you fix?

Phones, tablets, laptops and computers. The common jobs are cracked screens, batteries and back glass, and we also handle motherboard faults, phone unlocking and CCTV. Brands include iPhone, Samsung, OPPO, vivo, Huawei, Lenovo, Nokia, Sony, Acer and HTC.

How much will my repair cost?

It depends on the make, the model and the part. Call or email us with those details and what's wrong, and ask for a price before you leave your device with us.

How long does a screen repair take?

It depends on the model and whether we have the part in. One customer had their screen fixed within half an hour, but call ahead and we'll tell you what to expect for yours.

Do I need to book?

Give us a call before you come in. That way we can check we've got the right part for your device and that someone's at the counter.

Do you sell cases and screen protectors?

Yes. We keep a wall of cases and tempered glass for iPhone, Samsung and OPPO models at the shop.

Where are you, and who do you look after?

We're at 39 Jellicoe Street, Te Puke. We also look after customers from Pāpāmoa, Paengaroa, Pukehina and Rangiuru.
